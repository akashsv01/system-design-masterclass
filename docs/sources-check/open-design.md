## Not covered / left unchecked
- `gof`, `headFirst`, `posd`, `effJava`, `effJavaCreate` stay `s()` (books not read). Interpreter (no Guru page) is unchecked.
- SOLID’s S, O, I and D have no reachable primary source; L and separation of concerns are covered by `mit6031`.
- `uml` cites only `gof`, and no source read here teaches UML notation (Refactoring.Guru’s structure diagrams use it without explaining it). Proposal: find an open UML reference, or drop the citation.
- `responsibilities`: “ask which object has the information” (information expert) and “tell, don’t ask” are not in any source read.
- `cohesion-coupling`: “callers must call methods in a specific order → shallow interface” is not in the extract (Ch. 4–5).
- LLD case studies (`lld-*`) still cite `gof`; their designs were not checked in this batch.
